Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 24, 2026

TL;DR

  • pipedIn stores application data locally in your browser.
  • pipedIn does not operate a backend database or store your application records on its servers.
  • Your data leaves the browser only when you connect Google Drive and choose to save or restore a backup.
  • Google controls data stored in your Google Drive account under Google's own policies.
  • pipedIn does not sell your application data or use it for advertising.

This summary is for convenience and does not replace this Privacy Policy.

1. Information stored in pipedIn

pipedIn may store information you enter, including company and opportunity names, job titles, locations, salaries, URLs, employment details, application dates, notes, status history, interview details, ratings, and work-search log information. Avoid entering sensitive information unless necessary, including Social Security numbers or government identification numbers.

2. Local browser storage

By default, application data and preferences are stored in your browser using local storage. There is no pipedIn account or central application database for this data. Clearing browser or site data may delete it, and private browsing may delete it when the session ends. Anyone with access to your device or browser profile may be able to access it.

3. Google Drive

If you choose to connect Google Drive, pipedIn uses Google's authorization system and Drive API to save or restore backup files. The data may include your application records, interview information, notes, URLs, and other information in the selected backup.

pipedIn does not receive your Google password. Google authenticates your account and authorizes the selected Drive functionality. Google may independently collect, use, retain, and process information under its own privacy policy and terms. You can revoke access through your Google Account settings.

4. Information pipedIn collects directly

pipedIn does not intentionally collect application records on a central server, sell application data, or use it for advertising. The hosting provider, browser, Google, or infrastructure providers may process technical information such as IP address, request data, device information, or logs as part of delivering their services.

5. Cookies and tracking

pipedIn does not intentionally use advertising cookies or cross-site tracking technologies. Browser storage may remember application data and interface preferences.

6. How information is used and shared

Information is used to provide tracking and backup features, maintain interface preferences, operate and protect the service, and respond to support requests. Information may be processed by Google when you use Google Drive, hosting or security providers needed to operate the service, authorities when required by law, or a successor entity if the service is transferred.

7. Data retention and deletion

Local information remains in your browser until you delete it, clear site data, or replace it through the application. Google Drive backups remain there until you delete them. Deleting local pipedIn data does not automatically delete Google Drive backups. pipedIn does not control copies held by Google, your browser, your device, or downloaded backup files.

8. Security

pipedIn uses reasonable measures appropriate to the service, but no device, browser, network, or cloud storage service is completely secure. You are responsible for securing your device, browser profile, Google account, and downloaded backups.

9. Children

pipedIn is not directed to children under 13, or the applicable minimum age in your jurisdiction. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes and contact

We may update this Privacy Policy by posting a revised version with a new effective date. Privacy questions may be sent to ulk2yuj1c@mozmail.com.
